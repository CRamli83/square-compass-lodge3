Our Story

Kunitachi Lodge

Square and Compass Lodge No. 3, also known as Kunitachi Lodge, holds the distinction of being the third lodge accepted under the jurisdiction of the Most Worshipful Grand Lodge of Free and Accepted Masons of Japan.

Its origins trace to the post-war period, when Freemasonry was re-established in Japan. The lodge grew from a military lodge associated with the Tachikawa base and later established its home at the Kunitachi Masonic Center. Over more than five decades it has welcomed members of diverse nationalities and backgrounds, united by the Craft.

Today the lodge continues to meet monthly, primarily in Japanese with English also spoken, fostering brotherhood across cultures while supporting community activities in Kunitachi.

The Craft

Brotherly Love, Relief & Truth

I

Brotherly Love

We regard the whole human species as one family, regardless of race, station, or belief.

II

Relief

To soothe the unhappy, sympathize with their misfortunes, and restore peace to troubled minds.

III

Truth

The foundation of every virtue. To be good and true is the first lesson taught in Masonry.

The First Step

Membership

Freemasonry does not solicit members. Every man who becomes a Mason does so entirely of his own free will. “Ask, and the door shall be opened.”

Who May Petition?

  • A man of legal adult age
  • Of good moral character and reputation
  • Believes in a Supreme Being (of any faith)
  • Comes of his own free will — never invited or solicited
  • Able to support himself and his family

Membership is for men only, in accordance with traditional Masonic practice under the United Grand Lodge of England model followed by the Grand Lodge of Japan. Family events and affiliated organizations welcome wider participation.

The Path

  1. Make an inquiry
  2. Submit a petition
  3. Investigation by a committee
  4. Ballot of the lodge
  5. Conferral of the three degrees

Regular Communications

Meetings & Events

Square & Compass Lodge No. 3 holds its stated meetings on the first Sunday of each month at the Kunitachi Masonic Center.

Meetings typically begin in the early afternoon (around 13:00). A meal or fellowship often precedes the formal communication.

The lodge also participates in community activities, including children’s events in Kunitachi City, reflecting the principle of relief and service.

Visit / Inquire

Interested men of good character are welcome to inquire about attending an open event or learning more. Contact the lodge secretary for current details.
